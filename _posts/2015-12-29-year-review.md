With 2015 coming to a close, I took the time to recap what I had done this past year and came to the realization that this was my year of travel. I was fortunate enough to take a week long road-trip to Canada's East coast and fly to China for two weeks, which in hindsight is probably a personal record for distance traveled in a year. On top of that, I started working in Toronto at [Entertech](http://www.entertechsystems.com/) which increased my daily commute as well as the amount of walking I did in the city.
