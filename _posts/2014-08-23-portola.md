###Meta View & Their Space Glass

Random turn of events. I have been asked by [Steve Mann](https://www.google.ca/url?sa=t&rct=j&q=&esrc=s&source=web&cd=1&cad=rja&uact=8&ved=0CB0QFjAA&url=http%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FSteve_Mann&ei=W8f4U-uILY_esASE-YHQBA&usg=AFQjCNEmyU5NSBoX9kOWnviJXsaD4PD0nQ&sig2=tOqVr79Vl2exktLff9U28g&bvm=bv.73612305,d.cWc) to take a small trip down to California next week to check out [Meta's](https://www.spaceglasses.com/) [headquarters](http://www.495oldspanishtrail.com/) and play around with their first take on commercial holographic interfaces, [Spaceglasses](https://www.spaceglasses.com/).

After working with Steve Mann and his team, we were able to develop an augmented reality application (iOS/Android) which visualizes the veillances emitted from public cameras. We hope to further develop this platform for the Spaceglasses in the coming weeks.

While I am there, I’ll be checking out a few points of interests in the surrounding areas (Cupertino, Palo Alto etc), suggestions are welcomed :)
