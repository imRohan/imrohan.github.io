### Etymology, HTML5, CSS3, JS, Oh My!

Sometimes, finding inspiration for a personal project is difficult. This wasn't the case for me this time around. After reading a few articles on etymology (the study of the origin of words), I soon found myself aching to learn more about words which we do (or don't) use on a daily basis, and to showcase this new found knowledge in a creative way. Recently, I've been trying to familiarize myself with modern day web-design practices, and felt that it was about time I created a data visualization. What better way to incorporate the two then by creating a website dedicated to showcasing the origin and visualizing the usage of words. 

I chose Groovy purely for personal reasons.

### A week(ish) sprint dedicated to the word Groovy.

So, over the course of 10 days I worked on this little side project of mine. Although it really does not serve any great purpose, this was a great way to keep myself busy and to create something beautiful. I wanted to develop a SSW that was not only visually appealing, but also a platform which I could build upon at a later time.

Although the site currently only displays information relating to the word Groovy, I hope to add a library of words in the near future.

Try it out here, [Groovy, my foray into the world of Etymology](/projects/groovy)
